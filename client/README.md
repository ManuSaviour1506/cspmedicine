# 💊 Medease – Smart Medication Reminder Platform

Developed a community-driven web application aimed at assisting elderly individuals who frequently forget to take their medication. The solution enables caregivers to remotely manage medication schedules and ensures timely reminders are delivered directly to users.

Conducted field research by surveying over 50 households to understand real-world challenges faced by elderly individuals and caregivers. Based on these insights, designed a system that provides automated alerts and reminders, improving medication adherence and reducing health risks.

Built using modern web technologies, the platform focuses on usability, accessibility, and reliability, ensuring it can be easily adopted by non-technical users. Deployed the application on cloud infrastructure for seamless access and real-time functionality.

## 🚀 Overview

Medease is a web-based platform developed to address a real-world healthcare challenge: elderly individuals forgetting to take their medication on time.

The system allows caregivers to schedule reminders, which are then delivered directly to users, ensuring timely medication intake and improving overall health management.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Many elderly individuals face difficulty in remembering medication schedules, leading to:

- Missed doses  
- Health complications  
- Increased dependency on caregivers  

Traditional reminder methods are often unreliable or difficult to manage.

---

## 💡 Solution

Developed a smart reminder system where:

- Caregivers can schedule medication timings  
- Users receive automated alerts on their devices  
- The system ensures consistent and reliable reminders  

The platform is designed to be simple, accessible, and effective for non-technical users.
